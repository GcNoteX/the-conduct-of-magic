# the-conduct-of-magic

A shop-management, inspection and crafting game about running an enchanter's shop, made in Godot. Serve customers and inspect their goods by day, then enchant items by night.

Game design: [docs/design/game-design.md](docs/design/game-design.md)

Story: [docs/story/story-bible.md](docs/story/story-bible.md)

## Running the inscribing prototype
Open the project in Godot 4.7 and run it (F5). The main scene is the inscribing table (`inscribing/inscribing_table.tscn`), set up with the demo player in `inscribing/demo/demo_setup.tres`.

- **Place an enchantment:** pick it in the list, then click the item. Scroll or press R to rotate.
- **Draw a line:** press on a node and drag through neighbouring cells to another node. A line fills every cell it passes, so lines can't cross; move back along the line to undo cells. Lines can join magic nodes to each other to build a chain.
- **Insert a material:** drag it from the list onto an enchantment node. Nodes that accept it get a green ring. A planned material leaves your inventory and is held by that item until you take it back out.
- **Take back planned things:** right click a material, a line, then an enchantment. Esc drops what you're holding or cancels a line.
- **Commit:** commits every enchantment that is fully active. Its materials are used up then.
- **Disenchant:** pick an item with carvings (Demo sword #3 arrives pre-carved) and press Disenchant above the board. Each committed line shows its rune cost. Double-click a material to dissolve it for runes, click lines to plan cutting them, then click an enchantment node to pull it out once its whole chain is cut. Your runes are in the bar above the board, the plan is on the right (a step you can't afford shows red), and right click or Undo takes back the last step. Commit pulls out the planned enchantments and frees their cells.

Tests: `godot --headless --path . res://tests/test_inscription_board.tscn` and `res://tests/test_disenchant_board.tscn` (each exits 1 on failure).
Solvability (dev tool): `godot --headless --path . res://tools/solvability_checker.tscn` reports, for each carved demo item, whether its disenchant board can be cleared with the demo inventory, the fewest materials to dissolve and a cut order that works. Its test is `res://tests/test_solvability_checker.tscn`.
Building content in the editor: [build a map](docs/guides/build-a-map.md), [build an enchantment](docs/guides/build-an-enchantment.md).

Demo content is generated by `res://tools/build_demo_content.tscn`; rerunning it overwrites the demo `.tres` files.
